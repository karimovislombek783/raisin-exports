ABOUT THIS PROJECT

About

An independent student research project studying Uzbekistan's agriculture, international trade, and economic development.

This platform publishes independent, data-driven research on Uzbekistan's agricultural export sector — starting with dried and fresh fruit, and expanding over time to cover vineyard productivity, regional agricultural output, and broader trade dynamics.

Each dossier pairs original data analysis — drawn from sources like UN Comtrade and Uzbekistan's State Statistics Committee (stat.uz) — with an interactive chart set, so the underlying numbers are as explorable as the write-up itself.

This is a solo, self-directed student project. It isn't affiliated with any government body, university department, or trade organization, and it doesn't claim official or comprehensive coverage — it's an ongoing effort to make Uzbekistan's trade and agricultural data more legible, one dossier at a time.

The current focus areas are: Uzbekistan's raisin export trail (published), fresh grape export growth, and regional vineyard productivity — with more topics planned as the research continues.
